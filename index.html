1. Abstract & Overview

In the era of Ambient Intelligence, facial emotion recognition must often occur locally on mobile or IoT devices to ensure privacy and low latency. LightCBAMNet is a "Tiny" model optimized for these constraints.

Key Innovations include the Phish Activation ($x \cdot \tanh(\text{GELU}(x))$) and DCWP Modules which minimize FLOPs while maintaining feature richness.

2. Architecture Breakdown

  • Stem: Initial 7x7 convolution with stride 3 for rapid downsampling.
  • DCWP Layers: Depthwise-pointwise convolutions for efficient feature extraction.
  • CBAM Block: Channel and Spatial attention to focus on key facial landmarks.

3. Setup & Implementation

To train the model on your dataset, use the following command:

python train.py --data_dir images --epochs 20 --batch_size 32 --lr 0.001
